How explanations work

Each individual assessment's "Why?" explanation uses SHAP (SHapley Additive exPlanations) for tree-based models when the shap package is installed. If it is not available in your environment, a clearly labeled fallback based on overall model feature importance is used instead. Explanations describe model-estimated risk, not a borrower's actual character or guaranteed repayment behaviour.
